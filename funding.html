Funding and paying for care

Guidance on the different ways to fund care and support.

Your funding options

🏥

NHS Continuing Healthcare

Fully funded care

For those with significant ongoing health needs, the NHS may cover all care costs.

  • Complex medical needs
  • Intense nursing requirements
  • Unstable health conditions
🏛️

Local authority funding

Means-tested

Council support for those with savings below a certain threshold.

  • Care needs assessment
  • Financial assessment
  • Direct payments available
💷

Welfare benefits

Attendance Allowance · PIP

Financial support regardless of savings.

  • Attendance Allowance (over 65)
  • Personal Independence Payment (under 65)
  • Carer's Allowance
💰

Self-funding

Private pay

Flexible packages for those arranging their own care.

  • Hourly care
  • Live-in care packages
  • Respite care

Frequently asked questions

What is NHS Continuing Healthcare?

A package of care funded entirely by the NHS for those with a primary health need.

Can I get help if I own my home?

For care at home, your home is usually disregarded. For care homes, it may be included in the means test.

How long do assessments take?

Typically 4-6 weeks, but can be faster in urgent cases.
