Frequently asked questions

What is NHS Continuing Healthcare?

A package of care funded entirely by the NHS for those with a primary health need.

Can I get help if I own my home?

For care at home, your home is usually disregarded. For care homes, it may be included in the means test.

How long do assessments take?

Typically 4-6 weeks, but can be faster in urgent cases.
